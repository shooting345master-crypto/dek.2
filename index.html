01 — The club

A properly equipped floor,
not a shed with dumbbells.

Falcon Fitness opened in Kestopur on 15 August 2020. It is a unisex multigym club built for everyday training — air conditioned throughout, with premium strength and cardio equipment, filtered drinking water, clean bathrooms and free parking right at the building.
