2. Membership and admission

  • A one-time admission fee of ₹500 applies to the monthly General membership only.
  • All longer durations (3, 4, 6 and 12 months) carry no admission fee.
  • Membership is personal and may not be transferred to another person.
  • You must be 18 or older, or have a parent or guardian's consent, to join.

3. Prices

The rate card is shown on the Membership section to signed-in members, and is the price in force at the time of joining. Prices may change for new memberships; an active membership is not repriced mid-term. Visitors who are not signed in can see the plans listed without rates and can ask us for the current card.

4. Member accounts

Members are given a login so they can see their own membership, payments and attendance. Keep your password to yourself: anything done through your account is treated as done by you. Tell us at once if you think someone else has your password and we will reset it. We may disable an account when a membership lapses; your history is kept and comes back when you rejoin.

5. Payment

Membership fees are paid at the club by cash, UPI or card. We do not take payment through this website. A receipt will be issued for every payment — please keep it.

6. Refunds

  • Membership fees are non-refundable once your membership period has begun, including where a membership is revoked for uploading a receipt that was not a genuine payment of your own.
  • If you cancel before your first session, a full refund is given.
  • If the club permanently closes, any unused whole months of a prepaid membership are refunded on a pro-rata basis.
  • Day passes are non-refundable once the session has started.
  • Personal training sessions that are not used within the purchased period expire at the end of that period.

7. Freezing a membership

Memberships may be frozen for medical reasons or travel, for a minimum of 15 days and a maximum of 60 days, by speaking to us in person. A freeze must be requested before the period it covers begins.

8. Conduct and safety

  • Re-rack your weights and wipe down equipment after use.
  • Shoes must be worn on the training floor.
  • Follow coach instructions and do not attempt lifts beyond your ability without supervision.
  • We reserve the right to end a membership without refund for abusive behaviour, wilful damage to equipment, or bringing prohibited items onto the premises.

9. Risk and liability

Physical training carries inherent risk. By joining, you confirm you are fit to exercise and have declared any relevant medical conditions to us. If you have a heart condition, uncontrolled blood pressure, are pregnant, or are recovering from surgery, get medical clearance first and tell us before training. The club is not liable for injury resulting from exercising against medical advice or from misuse of equipment.

10. Property

Please do not leave valuables in the changing area. The club is not responsible for lost or stolen personal property.

11. Opening hours

Falcon Fitness is open every day from 6:30 am to 11:00 pm, including Sundays and public holidays. The website shows the current status live. We may close the club temporarily for maintenance, festivals or emergencies, and will give notice where possible.
