2. What we collect

When you submit the enquiry form on this website, we collect:

  • Your name — so we know who to ask for.
  • Your phone number — so we can call you back.
  • Your email address — optional, only if you choose to give it.
  • Which package you're interested in and any message you write.

We also record an anonymised, salted hash of your IP address purely to stop automated spam submissions. The raw IP address is never stored.

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not use third-party analytics, and we do not set marketing cookies on this website.

3. Why we collect it

For one purpose: to reply to your enquiry and answer your questions. We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone.

4. If you have a member account

Members can sign in to see their own membership, payments and attendance. For that we store your name, email address, phone number, plan, start and end dates, and payment and attendance records. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash — nobody at the club, including the owner, can read your password.

Seeing your own prices requires signing in; that is why rates are not shown to visitors who are not members. We never sell or share this data, and we do not use it for advertising. Ask us to close your account and we will delete the login and personal details; payment and attendance history that we are required to keep for accounting is retained, anonymised where possible.

5. Membership registration, receipts and your phone number

If you pay at the club and then register on this website, we also collect:

  • your mobile number, so we can send you a one-time code;
  • an image of your payment receipt — a screenshot from your UPI app or a photo of the paper receipt;
  • the amount, date and method of that payment, and the UPI reference if you give it;
  • the result of the automatic check we run on that image (a score and a short list of what the check found), so the owner can review it accurately.

Who can see your receipt image: exactly two people — you, and the club owner. It is stored in a private folder, is never linked from any public page, and the page that serves it refuses everyone else. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone.

The automatic image check. When you upload a receipt, our own software looks at the image for signs that it is a real, unedited picture — whether it is blank, whether it carries an editing app's stamp, whether the compression looks tampered with — and, where available, reads the amount and date off it. It runs on the club's own server. Your image is not sent to any outside service or AI provider for this. The result is a screening aid shown to the owner, who makes the actual decision.

We do not use your phone number for marketing. We use it to send verification and password-change codes. We do not keep a copy of any code after it has been used or has expired, and codes are stored only as a one-way hash.

6. Your consent

We only record an enquiry when you tick the consent box on the form. You can withdraw consent at any time by calling us or asking us to delete your details.

7. How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for a maximum of 12 months, or until you ask us to delete them — whichever comes first.

Membership records, including the receipts and payment details behind them, are kept for as long as your membership exists and afterwards for as long as the law requires us to keep accounting records. One-time codes are deleted once used or expired, and in any case within 24 hours.

8. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete it entirely.

To do any of these, call {{ b.phone }} or message us on Instagram at @{{ b.instagram }}. We will act on a deletion request within 7 working days.

9. Security

Enquiry and member data are stored on a private database with restricted access, and passwords are hashed. Receipt images sit in a private folder on the same server, are served only to the member who uploaded them and to the owner, and are sent to the browser without caching. No card or bank details are ever collected on this website — membership payments are taken in person at the club, and only a record that a payment was made is kept here for your account.

10. Children

This website is not directed at children under 18. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian contact us on your behalf.
